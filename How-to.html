Talking Clock & Alarm — How To

App version 1.0

Welcome

This guide explains how to use Talking Clock & Alarm: talking clock voice announcements, alarms, focus timers, and settings.

App overview

The app has four main areas from the bottom navigation bar:

  • Clock — Default tab. Current time and Talking Clock.
  • Alarm — Manage alarms. Opens automatically only while an alarm is ringing.
  • Focus — Pomodoro-style work/break timers.
  • Settings — Notifications, sound, vibration, talking-clock behavior, and dark mode.

Configure Settings first so notifications, sound, and vibration behave the way you want.

Settings

Always announce talking clock

Off (default): Standard talking-clock behavior. Announcements may be missed if the phone is left still on a table for a long time.

On: Uses scheduled exact alarms so voice time announcements continue even when the phone is idle on a surface.

Notifications, Sound, Vibration, Dark mode

Control alarm/timer notifications, audible output, vibration, and theme. Sound must be on for talking-clock voice announcements.

💡 Tip: Do Not Disturb and in-call state are respected — alarms and talking clock will not play sound during DND or calls.

Talking Clock

  1. Open Clock from the bottom bar.
  2. Set interval and quiet hours.
  3. Start the Talking Clock for background voice announcements.

Voice announcements only play when Sound is enabled in Settings. Quiet hours and Do Not Disturb still apply.

Alarms

  1. Open Alarm and tap +.
  2. Set time, message, optional talking alarm, and repeat days.
  3. Save the alarm.

When an alarm rings, tap the notification to open the app on the Alarm tab and dismiss it there.

⚠️ Note: Normal app launch opens the Clock tab. The app switches to the Alarm tab only while an alarm is actively ringing.

Focus timers

  1. Open Focus and create a timer.
  2. Tap Start, then use Pause/Resume/Stop as needed.
