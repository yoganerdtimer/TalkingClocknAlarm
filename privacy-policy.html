Privacy Policy

Talking Clock & Focus Timer · App version 1.3 · Last updated: September 7, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the Talking Clock & Focus Timer app ("the App") handles information when you use it on your Android device. The App is offered on Google Play and is intended to comply with applicable privacy requirements, including Google Play's data safety and policy requirements.

1. Data collection and use

We do not operate our own servers for this App. Alarms, timers, and settings you create stay on your device (see Section 2), except as described below for ads and purchases.

The free version may show advertisements via Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and process device and advertising identifiers, IP address, and interaction data according to Google's policies. You can remove ads permanently with a one-time in-app purchase processed by Google Play Billing.

The App uses the INTERNET permission to load ads and complete purchases. We do not sell your personal data.

We do not:

  • Collect your name, email, or phone number ourselves
  • Run our own analytics or crash-reporting SDKs beyond what Google Play / AdMob may use
  • Use ads or purchases for purposes other than showing ads and removing them

2. Data stored on your device

The App stores the following data only on your device:

  • Alarms — Times, messages, repeat days, and talking-alarm preference (Room database)
  • Focus timers — Names, work/break durations, cycle counts, and runtime state (Room database and SharedPreferences)
  • Talking Clock settings — Interval, quiet hours, on/off state, speech options, and optional "always announce" mode (SharedPreferences)
  • App settings — Notifications, sound, vibration, dark mode, headphone-only speech routing, whether ads have been removed, and in-app review prompt state (SharedPreferences)

Alarm and timer data is used solely to run the App's features and is not uploaded by us. Ad and purchase processing is handled by Google as described in Section 1.

2.1 Advertising and remove-ads purchase

Interstitial ads may appear when you open the App, switch tabs (subject to a cooldown), start a focus timer, or add/edit an alarm or timer. Ads are not shown while an alarm is ringing or after you purchase Remove ads.

Remove ads is a one-time non-consumable purchase. Prices are set in Google Play by country (approximately USD 5.99 in the United States, Canada, Europe, Australia, Singapore, Japan, and South Korea, and approximately USD 2.99 elsewhere; local currency and taxes may apply). Purchase status is restored via Google Play when you reinstall or change devices while signed into the same Play account.

See Google's policies: Google Privacy Policy and AdMob / Play Billing documentation.

2.2 Ratings and feedback

Rate on Google Play (Settings → Feedback & ratings) uses Google Play’s In-App Review API. The review dialog is shown by Google; we do not receive your star rating or review text directly unless you choose to post it publicly on Google Play.

The App may occasionally prompt for a rating after meaningful use (for example completing a focus timer session or dismissing an alarm), subject to eligibility rules stored locally on your device (launch count, days since install, and cooldown between prompts). You can always rate manually from Settings.

Send Feedback opens your email app with a category (Bug, Feature Request, General), your message, and basic device diagnostics (app version, Android version, device model). Email is sent from your device to siddhidatriapps@gmail.com; we do not operate a separate feedback server.

3. Android permissions

The App requests permissions only as needed for alarm-clock and productivity features:

  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS) — Alarm, talking-clock, and focus-timer notifications
  • Exact alarm (SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM / USE_EXACT_ALARM) — Fire user-set alarms on schedule using AlarmManager.setAlarmClock. If exact alarms are not allowed by the system, the App falls back to inexact scheduling where possible. Optional “Always announce talking clock” uses standard scheduled alarms (not exact-alarm APIs).
  • Vibrate (VIBRATE) — Vibration for alarms and timer notifications when enabled
  • Foreground service (FOREGROUND_SERVICE / FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK) — Keep alarm ringing and talking-clock voice announcements running while active. Both foreground services declare the media playback type because they play ringtone and text-to-speech audio.
  • Full-screen intent (USE_FULL_SCREEN_INTENT) — When a user alarm fires, the App may show alarm dismiss UI over the lock screen if the system grants this permission. If not granted, a high-priority notification is shown and the App opens the Alarm tab when you tap it. On Android 14 and later, you can manage this in system settings.
  • Bluetooth (BLUETOOTH_CONNECT) — Used only when you turn on Headphones only when connected in Settings, to route speech to wired or Bluetooth headphones. Not used to collect data.
  • Boot completed (RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED) — Reschedule alarms and restore talking clock after device restart or app update
  • Internet (INTERNET / ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE) — Load ads (AdMob) and process in-app purchases (Google Play Billing)

Permissions are not used by us to sell your data. Network access is required for ads and purchases.

3.1 Restricted permissions — why this App uses them

Google Play and Android restrict some permissions to apps whose core functionality requires them. Talking Clock & Focus Timer is an alarm-clock and timer utility. The permissions below are declared in the App manifest and used only for those features—not for analytics, ads, or background data collection.

1. USE_EXACT_ALARM (Android 13 / API 33 and above)

Android restricts USE_EXACT_ALARM to apps that need precisely timed, user-facing actions (for example alarm, calendar, or timer apps). On supported devices this permission is automatically granted at install when the App qualifies as an alarm-clock app.

Why we need it: User-set alarms must fire at the exact minute you choose. The App schedules them with AlarmManager.setAlarmClock, the Android API intended for alarm-clock apps. Optional “Always announce talking clock” uses separate scheduled alarms and does not rely on exact-alarm permissions.

On Android 12 (API 31–32), the App uses SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM instead; you may be prompted to allow exact alarms in system settings on those versions.

2. Foreground service types (Android 14 / API 34 and above)

From Android 14 onward, apps must declare a foreground service type for each foreground service and justify its use. Generic foreground services without a declared type are not permitted for these workloads.

Why we need it:

  • AlarmService — foregroundServiceType="mediaPlayback". Plays the alarm ringtone, vibration, and optional spoken alarm message until you dismiss the alarm.
  • TalkingClockService — foregroundServiceType="mediaPlayback". Plays on-device text-to-speech time announcements while the talking clock is enabled.

The App declares FOREGROUND_SERVICE and FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK. These services run only while an alarm is ringing or the talking clock is actively announcing—not for unrelated background work.

3. USE_FULL_SCREEN_INTENT — alarm UI on a locked device

On Android 14 and above, USE_FULL_SCREEN_INTENT is treated as a restricted permission. It is automatically granted to apps whose core function includes alarms or phone calls. Other app categories may require separate user consent in system settings.

Why we need it: When an alarm fires while your phone is locked, the App may show a full-screen alarm interface so you can see and dismiss the alarm without hunting through notifications. This permission is not used for marketing, overlays, or non-alarm content.

If full-screen intents are disabled on your device, the alarm still rings and appears as a high-priority notification; open the App or tap the notification to reach the Dismiss button on the Alarm tab. You can re-enable full-screen alarms in Android settings if you want the lock-screen experience.

4. Your control on Android 14+

Even when the permission is declared for a legitimate alarm app, Android lets you manage it: Settings → Apps → Talking Clock & Focus Timer → (Alarms & reminders / Full screen intents) (wording varies by device). Denying full-screen intent does not remove the App or your saved alarms; it only changes how the alarm appears when the screen is off or locked.

We do not use full-screen intents for talking-clock announcements or focus timers—only for active user alarms where a prominent dismiss UI is appropriate.

4. Text-to-speech (TTS)

The App uses your device's built-in Text-to-Speech engine for the Talking Clock and talking alarms. Speech is generated on your device. The App does not send alarm messages or other text to our servers. Third-party TTS engines (for example Google Text-to-Speech) may have their own terms if you install or use them separately.

5. Device backup

The App may participate in Android backup for some settings stored in SharedPreferences (for example talking-clock and app settings). Alarms and focus timers stored in the local database are excluded from cloud backup by the App's backup rules, so they are not restored automatically after reinstall unless you recreate them.

Backup is controlled by your device and Google account settings. We do not receive or store your backup data.

6. External links

Settings may open Help, How-to Videos (YouTube playlist), or this Privacy Policy in your device's web browser or YouTube app. Those destinations are opened externally; the App does not embed a WebView or send your alarm, timer, or settings data when you open them.

7. Do Not Disturb and system settings

When the device is in Do Not Disturb mode, the App may suppress alarm sound and skip starting an alarm session, depending on system rules. Focus-timer and talking-clock behavior may also respect Do Not Disturb and in-call state. Adjust Android sound, notification, exact-alarm, and full-screen-intent settings as needed for your use case.

8. Children's privacy

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. It is a utility app and does not target children.

9. Data security

Alarm, timer, and settings data stay in the App's private storage on your device; their security depends on your device settings (lock screen, encryption). We do not transmit that data ourselves. Ads and purchases may involve network traffic handled by Google AdMob and Google Play Billing, as described in Sections 1 and 2.1.
