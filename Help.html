Talking Clock & Focus Timer — Help

App version 1.3 · Last updated September 7, 2026

First-time setup

  1. Allow notifications when prompted (alarms, timers, and talking-clock alerts).
  2. On Android 12, allow exact alarms in system settings if asked — required for user-set alarms to fire on time.
  3. On first install, the app may add a sample weekday alarm and a sample Pomodoro timer so you can explore features. Edit or delete them anytime.
  4. Open Settings and confirm Sound, Notifications, and Vibration match how you want the app to behave.
  5. Set a test alarm 2–3 minutes ahead to verify ringtone, dismiss, and notifications work on your device.

Common issues

Alarm did not ring

  • Confirm the alarm toggle is on.
  • Check device time and timezone.
  • Allow exact alarms and disable aggressive battery optimization for this app.
  • After reboot, alarms reschedule automatically — open the app once if needed.

How to dismiss a ringing alarm

  • While an alarm is ringing, open the app — it switches to the Alarm tab automatically.
  • Tap the full-width Dismiss button on the alarm banner, or use Dismiss on the notification.
  • On a locked device, the alarm may open over the lock screen if your phone allows full-screen alarms.

Talking clock is quiet on the table

  • This is expected with the default setting.
  • Turn on Always announce talking clock in Settings for more reliable announcements when the phone is still.
  • Ensure Sound is enabled and quiet hours are not blocking announcements.

App opens on the wrong tab

  • Normal launch opens the Clock tab.
  • While an alarm is ringing, opening the app (or tapping the alarm notification) opens the Alarm tab.

No sound

  • Enable Sound in Settings.
  • Check Do Not Disturb and Alarm volume on your phone.
  • Ensure Text-to-Speech is installed on the device (e.g. Google TTS).

Ads or Remove ads purchase

  • The free version may show short interstitial ads (with a cooldown between shows).
  • Ads are not shown while an alarm is ringing, or after you purchase Remove ads.
  • Purchase or restore from Settings → Premium. You must install from Google Play (not a sideloaded debug build) and use a Play account that can buy or is a license tester.
  • If the price does not appear, wait a moment for the store to connect, then try again.

Feedback or rating

  • Settings → Feedback & ratings → Rate on Google Play opens Google’s in-app review when available.
  • Send Feedback opens your email app with a category (Bug, Feature Request, General) and optional message.
  • The app may occasionally suggest a rating after you complete a focus session or dismiss an alarm (subject to Google Play rules and usage thresholds).

Frequently asked questions

Q: Does the app work offline?

A: Yes for alarms, talking clock, and focus timers. Internet is needed for ads, in-app purchases, Play Store ratings, and when you open Help, Privacy Policy, or How-to Videos in the browser / YouTube.

Q: Why is the talking clock silent when my phone is on the table?

A: With the default setting, the in-app timer loop may miss announcements when the device is idle. Turn on Always announce talking clock in Settings for scheduled background announcements.

Q: Which tab opens when I launch the app?

A: The Clock tab is the default. The Alarm tab opens automatically only while an alarm is ringing.

Q: How do I remove ads?

A: Open Settings → Premium → Remove ads (one-time purchase). Use Restore purchase after reinstall if you already bought it on the same Play account.

Q: How do I send feedback or report a bug?

A: Open Settings → Feedback & ratings → Send Feedback. Choose a category, write your message, and send via email. If no email app is installed, contact siddhidatriapps@gmail.com directly.
