Talking Clock & Alarm — Help

App version 1.0

First-time setup

  1. Allow notifications when prompted.
  2. Allow exact alarms in Android settings if asked.
  3. Configure Sound, Notifications, and Vibration in app Settings.
  4. Set a test alarm 2–3 minutes ahead to verify it works.

Common issues

Alarm did not ring

  • Confirm the alarm toggle is on.
  • Check device time and timezone.
  • Allow exact alarms and disable aggressive battery optimization for this app.

Talking clock is quiet on the table

  • This is expected with the default setting.
  • Turn on Always announce talking clock in Settings for reliable announcements when the phone is still.
  • Ensure Sound is enabled and quiet hours are not blocking announcements.

App opens on the wrong tab

  • Normal launch opens the Clock tab.
  • While an alarm is ringing, opening the app (or tapping the alarm notification) opens the Alarm tab.

No sound

  • Enable Sound in Settings.
  • Check Do Not Disturb.
  • Ensure Text-to-Speech is installed on the device.

Frequently asked questions

Q: Does the app work offline?

A: Yes for alarms, talking clock, and focus timers. An internet connection is only needed when you open Help or Privacy Policy links in the browser.

Q: Why is the talking clock silent when my phone is on the table?

A: With the default setting, the in-app timer loop may miss announcements when the device is idle. Turn on Always announce talking clock in Settings for reliable scheduled announcements.

Q: Which tab opens when I launch the app?

A: The Clock tab is the default. The Alarm tab opens automatically only while an alarm is ringing.
